Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2025-12-20
  Major Features:
    - Initial release of Scarecrow mod
    - Stationary wooden box with 4x4 equipment grid
    - Grid-snapped placement like buildings
    - Full inserter compatibility for item input/output
    - Uses standard building collision mask - prevents blueprint overlap
    - No fuel requirements - operates with void energy source
    - Cannot be entered as vehicle - functions purely as stationary storage with equipment grid
  Info:
    - Crafted from 1 wooden chest + 5 wood
    - 16 inventory slots (same as wooden chest)
    - Equipment grid supports armor category equipment
    - Appears and behaves like a building, not a vehicle
